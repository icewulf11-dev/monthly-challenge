Privacy policy

My Mini Painting Challenge (Android app) · Last updated 18 October 2026

My Mini Painting Challenge is made by Sophie, an independent developer in the UK. In short: the app has no accounts, no adverts and no analytics, and everything you put into it stays on your phone.

What the app stores

The app saves these things on your phone only:

  • the paint ranges you choose and your settings
  • your projects: the paints rolled, mini names, dates, notes and progress
  • before and after photos you take or choose for a project
  • badges you have earned

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. We have no server that collects it, so we cannot see it.

Photos

When you tap Take photo, the app opens your phone's own camera app. When you tap Choose, it opens the phone's picker so you can choose a picture. The app only gets the picture you pick, and it keeps it inside the app on your phone.

Internet use

The app goes online only to download small public files with the latest seasonal challenges, release day challenges and event badges. These files are hosted on GitHub. The app sends no personal information with these requests. Like any website, GitHub can see your device's IP address when the file is downloaded; see GitHub's privacy statement.

Notifications

If you switch on reminders, the app schedules them on your phone. No notification service or server is involved, and you can turn them off at any time in the app or in your phone's settings.

Sharing

When you tap Share, Save image or Copy image, the app makes a picture and hands it to the app or place you choose, such as Instagram or your gallery. It only does this when you ask. What happens after that is covered by the app you share to.

Backups

You can make a backup file from the app and save it wherever you choose. Android may also back up the app's data to your own Google account, if you have Android backup switched on. We cannot see either backup.

Event codes

If you type an event code to unlock a badge, the app checks it on your phone. The code is not sent anywhere.

Children

The app is meant for people aged 13 and over. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Deleting your data

Everything is on your phone, so you are in control. Delete a project in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data. Remember to delete any backup files you made too.

Changes

If the app ever starts handling information differently, this page will be updated first and the date at the top will change.
